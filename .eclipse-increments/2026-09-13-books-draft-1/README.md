# Draft 1 of the four books — kept, not replaced

Parked here 2026-09-13 before the second drafts, per the habit this directory exists for: a losing or
superseded increment gets a shelf, a date and a name, and is never called a mistake.

These are the drafts the three cold readers actually read. Every finding in
`advice/` and every mutation in the authors' sheets points at *these* bytes. When a sheet says a voice
changed, this is the before.

`sha256sum -c MANIFEST.sha256` re-proves them.

## The signatures

Twelve `.sig` files arrived here with their bytes. They were made on the host at 22:14 on 2026-09-12,
over *these* round-1 files, and they sit beside them because a signature that does not travel with the
bytes it covers is worse than no signature — it looks like cover for something it never saw.

`book/games/` holds the round-2 drafts. Those are different bytes and carry no signature.
