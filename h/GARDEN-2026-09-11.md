## JUDGED [MINE], each with its check

- *Worn means curated.* I moved into the garden only what the app already wears, byte-identical, and
  left unworn mints on the floor. If the garden is bless-by-signature only, say so and the five
  mint-born residents go back to the floor (copies, nothing lost).
- *Vendor list.* docx/pdf/pptx/xlsx/skill-creator/import-memory/morning are Anthropic's, judged from
  their text (no author line; proprietary LICENSE on docx). If one is yours, `--vendor` takes a
  shorter list and it turns INSTALLED-ONLY at once.
- *Canon by reference, not copy.* `make dist` reads `c4/dist/skills` as a root instead of copying
  14 skills into the garden, because `c4cs make-skills` regenerates them from decks. If you'd rather
  every minted skill sit in one directory, one `cp -r` per canon skill and the root drops out of the
  Makefile.
- *GNU make.* The Makefile is headed `.POSIX:` but the new INSTALLED line uses `$(if …)` and
  `$(wildcard …)` — GNU functions, as the file's own comment says it is a GNU-style Makefile. On a
  strictly POSIX make, pass `INSTALLED=…` by hand.
- *Words.* bin/skill calls `$SEAT/skills` the forge and the app store live; sg1ck calls them the
  garden and the worn copy, and says so in its header. One word each would be better; that is a
  rename in one tool, your pick which.
