## The gap (what was missing, and where you were "sorta" stuck)
The blessed **SEMAPHORES v1.1** — SEAT · MANIFEST-MEMORY · STRANDED · GROW — govern one
axis: **where a note lives and whether it stranded**. None of them says the thing a
preference needs: **"this is in force, and it must fire this session."** A preference
today (prefers-concise, prefers-mingw-paths) is honored softly — by me reading MEMORY.md
and remembering. Soft = not a *reliable* semaphore. That missing axis is the stuck point.

## Why ccspt is the right suspicion
ccspt is a **consent-&-provenance layer** with a two-gate lifecycle:
`receipt (lenient, STATUS=unverified) → store → publish (strict, re-hash, attestation)`,
consent a signed statement **bound to the payload's sha256**. That lifecycle *is* the
missing axis. It doesn't govern location (SEAT does); it governs **standing** —
advisory → verified → in force. Map it onto preferences and the reliability problem
dissolves.

## Proposal — a PREF semaphore (raise-cheap, lower-by-ceremony)
A new signal family, one axis orthogonal to SEAT/STRANDED:
- **CAPTURED** ◆ — Mentat noted it without asking (note-taking-directives = ccspt's
  lenient receipt; STATUS advisory). Raising is free.
- **IN-FORCE** ● — blessed by you + hash-pinned (ccspt's strict publish gate). This is
  the reliable semaphore: loaded and honored *every* session; checkable, not vibes.
- **CONFLICT** ■ — two in-force prefs disagree (e.g. "concise" vs "explain fully here");
  parks for you, never auto-resolved.
- **SUPERSEDED** ◇ — a newer pref retired it (ccspt versioning); lowered, kept as history.

Consent stays lightweight per feature-no-math: I hand a ready-to-sign PREF block; you
bless; the math is available, never pushed.

## "Full operator" = the loop that runs this reliably
1. **Capture** without asking (note-taking-directives).
2. **Reseat** so nothing strands (seatkeeper — now installed; the floor).
3. **Mark** the PREF semaphore (CAPTURED → offer to bless → IN-FORCE).
4. **Load & honor** IN-FORCE prefs at session open, and **show** them (the PREFERENCES
   band, generated by scanning the seat → scoreboard.py; proven today).
5. **Verify honored** — an evalboard-style check: did the reply actually respect the
   in-force prefs? (the measuring half, later).
