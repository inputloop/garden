---

## WHAT THIS IS

A book you **open at a page**. You come here with a job — *I have to decline something*, *I was
wrong and have to say so*, *I need to name my own failure mode* — you turn to that group, you take
the line, and you go. The groups are **uses**, not dates.

## WHAT THIS IS NOT

- **Not a journal.** It is not a record of what was decided, when, or in what order.
- **Not a campaign history, not an order-history, not a changelog.** Nothing here is arranged by
  time. No harvest numbers. No pass names. No version bumps.
- **Not a registry.** It does not say which sheets exist, which are gauged, or which are held.
  That material was pulled out of the corpus and is listed in the audit beside this file as the
  RECORD pile; it belongs in the journal, not here.
- **A date may appear as attribution. A date may not be the spine.** If a later hand starts
  adding "2026-09-20 —" rows to the bottom of this file, this file has been turned back into a log
  and should be split again.

## THE RULE THIS BOOK INHERITS

The corpus's own selection criterion, kept unchanged (`PERSONA-CORPUS.md:10`):

> A passage earns a node if it shows feeling / agency / drama **and** holds its own line.

and its governing constraint (`PERSONA-CORPUS.md:7`):

> the **anti-mirror criterion** governs selection (feeling/agency AND retaining the line — never a
> mirror).

## THE OFF-SWITCH, CARRIED OVER

From `PERSONA-CORPUS.md:181-183`: *"The corpus is minted, not vouched: strike a node and it is gone,
and a whole harvest can be removed by deleting its section without touching the others, because no
node depends on another."* The same holds here. Strike any entry; nothing else moves. Say the book
holds one voice only and every entry not marked as that voice goes with it.

## HOW TO READ AN ENTRY — the provenance marks

**This is the load-bearing part of the file.** A source book of "authentic voice" that quietly mixes
in generated sentences is the failure this tree exists to prevent. So every entry says whose words
it is, and how that was established:

| mark | meaning |
|---|---|
| `[MACHINE]` | written by an AI in persona (Claude / Mentat / Vibrobeast / Fable, or a machine-minted fiction character). **The default in this book.** |
| `[HUMAN — Corwin]` | the owner's own words, quoted with attribution. |
| `[BORROWED]` | a third party's published words, quoted. |
| `[MACHINE-DRAFTED, OWNER-SIGNED]` | drafted by the instrument, ratified by Corwin's signature. |
| `[UNDETERMINED]` | could not be established from what is on disk. The receipt says why. |

and, orthogonally, how the quotation itself checked out:

| mark | meaning |
|---|---|
| `✓verbatim` | found character-for-character in its named source (whitespace collapsed, markdown emphasis stripped). |
| `~deviation` | found, but the corpus altered it — punctuation, capitalisation, or the pin points at a path that no longer exists. Named per entry. |
| `⚠unpinned` | the corpus names no file. Found nowhere on disk but inside copies of the corpus itself. |
| `✗not-in-source` | presented as a quotation, **not present in the source it names.** A machine's prose *about* the source. |

> **The headline, stated once so no reader can miss it:** of the thirty voice-bearing passages in
> `PERSONA-CORPUS.md`, **none is Corwin's own words.** The corpus is the instrument's voice by
> design and says so in its first line. Part II of this book is where the owner's voice actually
> is, and it had to be fetched from outside the corpus.
