# MODEL — skills, from the perspective of the people who share them
Stand-alone static model, 2026-09-05, Fable as Mentat (Vibrobeast, Cowork) for Corwin.
Built by sifting MAIN MEMORY into piles, running the grains against the Planning Palace
(`/f/claude/c4/q/retention/`), and building one **floor** per grain — finest first, each new
floor dropped above the last, references appended at the back with a doubly-linked
cross-reference to the original work. Viewpoint: **the skill-shaped behavior that users of
AI share** — where it must be Claude-specific (and why), where it reaches vendor-agnostic or
broad-vendor so our colors show, and how a distinct feature expresses itself respectfully into
the wider ecosystems of community as they understand them. Every floor answers one question:
**how can I serve most users most composability without bringing new risks I cannot explain?**
Guesses marked `[MINE, ~N%]` or with the hypothesis words. Copies: `/f/claude/AS4CE/AS4CE0001/h/`
(master), `/f/claude/c4/h/`. Palace files read; the concerns shelf excluded from ideation by rule.

## Floor 1 — coarsest grain: `composab` (0 hits in memory, 0 in the palace)

**Central thought at this grain:** the round question's own word has no room in either house.
Word-weighting the palace gives *check · rule · session · sweep* — the Sweep Rule's *"a concern
becomes a check that rides with the work"* — and in memory the nearest names for composability
are `self-contained-universe`, the delivery **tiers** (`magic-words-persona`: persona-trait · skill
· tool · UI; `tool-lifecycle`: Layer 0 filesystem → persona → skill → tool-CLI → bespoke UI), and
`hooks-to-knowledge` (a hook is to knowledge, so you may substitute). `[MINE, ~70%]` that these
three *are* our composability doctrine under other names; falsifier: Corwin names a fourth.

**What this floor says about shared skill-shaped behavior:** users of AI compose by **tier**, not
by API. A thing that ships as a paste-in file composes with every vendor; as a skill, with one
vendor's rack; as a tool, with a shell; as a UI, with nobody else. The palace's `pocket-mentat`
already names the three tiers it could ship in (*"a paste-in file, a Claude skill, or a signed
shareable"*, `skilltank-candidates.md:13`). **The composable form is the lowest tier that still
carries the check.**

**Claude-specific, and why it matters here:** the *skill* tier is Claude's (`Skill` tool, the
description-as-magic-word). The *paste-in file* tier is vendor-agnostic — today's `PROMPT.md` at
the experiment seat is one, and `operating-model-BASELINE.md` at `H:\ta` is another. **Our colors:**
C4's README says it in one line — *"No AI is required. That is the point."*

**Round question:** serve the most users by shipping at the paste-in tier first and the skill
tier second; the only new risk is that a paste-in file cannot *refuse* (no tool rides with it) —
explainable, and answered by shipping the tool beside it (this week's `bin/` finding at `H:\ta`).

## Floor 2 — grain R1: names an installed skill **and** carries "How to apply" (14 of 144 · 9.7%)

**Central thought (palace, 19 hits in 5 files):** the `[SKILL]` tag is *"a rung on the lineage"*
(`pins-flip-the-script.md:24`) and a FORT is *"a context anchor, like a skill, giving near-reach
extra force"* (`semaphores.md:6`). So in C4's own conception a skill is **a named authority you
can point at when you object** — not a capability, a *provenance*. The palace's ENHANCE list
(`skilltank-candidates.md`) is the working list of what should be folded into which skill, and its
first item is the same fold my Q11 asks for.

**What the 14 winners share:** every one is a note about *how to behave*, not what a thing is —
`named-skill-visible-dispatch`, `cannot-edit-live-skills`, `verify-mount-access-fresh-per-session`,
`handback-card-format`, `code-idiom-freedom`, `bubble-chamber-method`… Skill-shaped memory in this
shop is **conduct plus a receipt**, never a feature list. `[MINE, ~80%]` that this is the shape
users of AI actually share when they share skills: the *manners* of a tool, not its manual.

**Claude-specific:** the `named-skill-visible-dispatch` rule is only meaningful where a `Skill`
tool exists; the `[[wikilink]]` memory index is Cowork's. **Vendor-agnostic:** "How to apply" as
a required section is plain prose — any vendor's system prompt can carry it. **Respectful into
the ecosystem:** the GNU habit — a man page has a *DESCRIPTION* and *EXAMPLES* — is the general
form of "what it is + how to apply"; our skills should read like man pages to a stranger.

**Round question:** conduct rules compose across users freely (they are text); the risk that
cannot yet be explained is *enforcement* — a rule in a `SKILL.md` is a reader, not a gate
(Q166's lesson, generalized). Say so on every skill: *this is a manner, not a lock.*

## Floor 3 — grain R2: winners that also mention users / community / ecosystem (1 of 14)

**Central thought (palace, 18 hits, 17 of them in one file):** the user appears in the palace
almost only through the mp3 model `model-agents-rules-for-life.md` — *keep refocusing on the
user's intent* (:10); **learning-annotation** — *annotate each learning with the direct user
quote it traces to* (:21–22); *announce gently, esp. to a non-technical user* (:28); the lineage
walk ends at the *innermost user preference* (:30); **err-user-side-but-check-first** (:35);
**shortest-dialectic** — *the shortest story that leaves the user a question they must answer*
(:42); *never misdirect users/systems* (:49); **opportunity-space** — *no paradigm solves
community, fact, truth, temptation, fallibility* (:51); and when you can't meet the build, *end
with the single act the user can take to restart* (:69).

**What the one winner (`handback-card-format`) shares with them:** a hand-back is a leave-the-UI
card — WHEN / DO / CHECK / TELL-ME — *the single act the user can take*, made a form. That is the
palace's :69 line as a skill. `[MINE, ~75%]` the card format is the most composable artifact we
have: it needs no vendor, no tool, no key — a person and a terminal.

**Claude-specific:** the user quote that annotates a learning is, in Cowork, the chat transcript
— other vendors' users have no equivalent record unless they keep one. **Vendor-agnostic:** the
card, the direct quote, the shortest dialectic — all prose. **Respectful into the ecosystem:** the
free-software community already has this manner — a bug report that ends with *"to reproduce,
run X"* is a hand-back card; our skills should say so rather than rename it.

**Round question:** serving *most* users means serving the non-technical one the mp3 names —
announce gently, end with one act. Risk: a card can be wrong and still be followed; explainable,
and answered by the CHECK line (every card carries its own falsifier).

## Floor 4 — finest grain: winners that also mention publish / pub / travel / ship (0 of 1)

**The most clearly bad room:** nothing in skill-shaped memory that talks about users *also* talks
about shipping. The palace, by contrast, is full of it — 27 hits in 6 files, all in the **SHIP
fort**: `ship-dont-plead` (*a concern becomes a check that rides WITH the work or a named fork —
never a standing caution*, `semaphores.md:16`), `self-contained-universe` (*anything signed must
open itself, ship its tools+tests, stand alone*, :17), and the ten "right-now improvements"
(`context-work-improvements.md`): **lead with the OFF switch** (:17), a MARKER check **must carry a
negative control** (:22), an **honest degradation ladder** with a dial-1 SEE for every ABORT, a
**register ladder** — RECIPE has voice → README driest → the tool is man(1) (:5), **one runnable
step = one fenced block** (:8), **structure for grep, not color** (:9), and the meta line: *"this
feature is The Sweep Rule made physical"* (:57).

**What this floor teaches about shared skill-shaped behavior:** the community *receives* a skill
as a shipped thing, and every shipping rule above is vendor-agnostic — fenced blocks, grep-able
output, an off switch documented before the on-behavior, a tool that can go red. **Our skills
talk to users about conduct and to nobody about shipping**; the palace has the shipping rules
and no user in the room. The two halves of this shop's skill discipline live on different floors
and have not met. `[MINE, ~85%]` — falsifier: a skill in the rack whose SKILL.md carries both a
"How to apply" and a shipping section (off switch, negative control, one-block steps). Run, not
quoted: `grep -li 'off switch\|--prove-red' skills/*/SKILL.md` → **0 of 58** installed skills;
a softer test (a when-to-use section *and* any of ship / prove-red / off switch / negative
control) → **4 of 58**: consent-gate, ethicsminions, skilltank, takushi. So the two halves have
met in four rooms, and in none of them as a shipping *section*.

**Claude-specific, and where being choosy to use all of Claude matters:** sub-agents (the LENS
racks), the folder-request tool, the artifact/widget surfaces — these are how *we* verify a
skill before it ships; none of them ship *with* it, and they should not. **Vendor-agnostic:** the
shipped form is the self-contained universe: `SKILL.md` + `scripts/` + `fixtures` + `tests.sh
--prove-red` + `check.sh` for a stranger with no key — exactly what `pencil-your-own` and #184/#185
built this week. **Respectful into the ecosystem:** ship it the way GNU ships — a `README`, a
`COPYING` (the ceremony C4 has not yet performed and ccspt's README says is still to do), a
`make check` that can fail, and no dependence on any vendor to run it.

**Round question:** this floor *is* the answer — composability without unexplainable risk is the
self-contained universe: the check rides with the work, the off switch is documented first, the
degradation ladder is honest, and a stranger can run `--prove-red`. The one risk I cannot yet
explain to a non-Claude user is the *reader-side* sha (Q12): a skill can prove its own bytes,
not that the model read them.

## Side rooms — the swings that had hits

- **S3 `risk` (6 in memory, all in the less-likely pile; 4 in the palace):** in memory, risk is
  spoken of only in governance notes (`guard-surface-philosophy`, `federation-expansion-gate`,
  `multi-user-transparency-model`, `lens-discipline`, `pmo-supervisor`, `blessed-principle`); in
  the palace it is **the turnaround tension** — *at the in→out turnaround, tension rises → hurried
  answer risk → fall back to the pre-prepared concern list* (`pins-flip-the-script.md:11`). And at
  `H:\ta` the word is *gated*: nothing is a risk until measured. So across the three houses,
  "risk" is either a governance word, a tension to pre-empt, or a measured cell — never a skill's
  word. `[MINE, ~70%]` a skill that says "risk" without a cell is using the governance sense and
  should say `concern`.
- **S2 `vendor|agnostic|MCP` (11 in memory, 9 in the less-likely pile; 0 in the palace):** the
  vendor question lives in the wire/Drive/MCP notes and nowhere in the palace — the palace is
  about *us*. The stance that shows our colors is at C4's root, not in its rooms: *"Nothing here
  needs an AI to use… C4 exists to restrict an AI within the context of the system"*
  (`c4/README.md`). Every skill we ship should be able to say that sentence about itself.
