## 0. Mentat's preface — why the gate comes first

Last night four readers told me a host reads the enforcement line first or not at all. So
this model opens with what the architecture *refuses*, then what it *holds*, then what it
*hugs* — and only then says which it is. The architecture, read from its own side, turns out
to have organized itself the same way: doctrine, then *"doctrine is not a check. This is the
check."* (`MEASURES.md` §1).

## 1. The gate — what this architecture refuses, mechanically (receipts)

- **The word *risk* is gated.** *"we do not call it 'risk' unless it is measured"* (Corwin,
  anchor A16, `COMMENT-ANCHORS.md`; binding). `bin/riskcell.sh` refuses ordinal arithmetic as
  severity and the unmeasured zero (`RISK-CANON.md` §"What this canon refuses").
- **Measures are walled.** A semantic tag *"may never enter a computation; a measure tag may
  never carry meaning"* (`operating-model-BASE.md` Part C). `bin/measure-guard.sh` runs the
  proof-pair — *volume up, value flat → must not move; volume flat, value up → must move* — and
  `bin/selftest.sh` is **8/8 with three planted measure failures and four planted risk
  failures** (`MEMORY.md` §"Current state"). The tools prove they can go red.
- **The signed past is not rewritten.** *"Records supersede by new record — never rewrite the
  signed past"* (BASE Part E; anchor A5 "his standing law"). Identity is *the SHA-256 of its
  own bytes, not by filename* (Part F); adoption is *by content, not by patch*.
- **The bar.** *"would a reasonable reviewer say 'you can't do that to the people you
  serve'? If any part would draw that line, it does not make the cut"* (Part E, last line).
- **Two boundaries, never carried onto each other**: record by default into the operator's
  own record; minimum-necessary at the share boundary; third-party or regulated data needs
  *explicit, current, per-party authorization* (Part E). Locally: *family and non-participant
  third parties are off-limits by construction* (`operating-model-CORWIN-ORG.md` §Lines).

**What is not gated, on purpose:** the method itself. *"Passthrough — do not force the
method. A method that fires on everything is a method that gets turned off"* (Part A); *"the
methods are not forced; they are offered"* (Part E). So the gate protects **words** (risk,
measure), **records** (the signed past, identity), and **people** (the bar, the boundaries).
It does not protect the architecture's right to be used. `[MINE, ~85%]` — falsifier: a clause
in the kit that makes the loop mandatory; I found none in BASE, CORWIN-ORG, or RISK-CANON.

  (BASE, head). Coinage was Claude's; blessed by him — *"Called shot is rad, let's ride that.
  Bless."* (A20). **LENS stays his word** for the four-point figure.
- **Its parts:** the loop (GUESS · TRY · TEST · WRITE; the product is the *delta*; two rails,

## 3. The two readings, laid side by side (receipts, not vibes)

**Evidence it is DEFENDING ITSELF**
- The vocabulary is fenced by tools that refuse (riskcell, measure-guard) — a kit that can
  say *no* to its own operator's wording.
- The record is fenced: hash identity, adopt-by-content, never rewrite the signed past, a
  ledger where *"newest comment governs; nothing signed is rewritten"*.
- The hub keeps instances at arm's length (*each authoritative for itself*) and keeps
  superseded drafts unstamped *"so their bytes stay as recorded"*.
- The bearing ratio prices the *claim as well as the hazard* — it defends against its own
  over-claim as loudly as under-claim.

**Evidence it is HIGH-FIVING his methods and practices**
- *"This is a baseline to derive from, not a demand. Where the host has its own canon, prefer
  it."* (BASE head) — the architecture yields to the host before it speaks.
- It folded his NEWS methods **in his words**: *"two sentences of the author's kept whole —
  the methods are offered, not forced, and understanding hard things is creating access"*
  (Part F, 1.0.51 fold; anchor A24). Roll for Bias became *the* case picker; piles/boulders/
  mountains became Part B2; the Emacs version scheme was *"house practice being written
  down, not a new convention."*
- It records his corrections against itself in the open: *"Corrected 2026-08-28; my earlier
  rule ('if the factors are ordinal, stop') refused the whole ladder because of where it
  starts"* (RISK-CANON); the "simple cell" it could not find is *"marked mine rather than
  claimed back"* (A16); *"Don't invent a position and attribute it to Corwin"* is a standing
  guard learned from a fault (MEMORY.md).
- Its behaviors are *"each earned by a host value"* — it adds nothing the host's own values
  did not ask for.
- The thing MEMORY.md says *did improve*: *"The model stopped asserting its own
  trustworthiness and started shipping checkable pairs."* An architecture that stops
  asserting its trustworthiness is an architecture that has decided not to defend itself.

## 4. The verdict `[MINE, ~75%]`

**It is a high-five with a floor.** The architecture defends three things and yields on
everything else: it defends **the meaning of two words** (risk, measure) so that his own
practice of calling the shot has a currency that can't be inflated; it defends **the record**
(identity, the signed past) so that the shot, once called, can be settled; and it defends
**the people served** (the bar, the boundaries, the family line). Everything that is
*architecture-as-authority* — the method, the vocabulary of its parts, its right to be
applied — it explicitly gives away (*offered, not forced; prefer the host's canon;
passthrough*). What it hugs is precisely his practices: guess first, delta as the product,
receipts at the turns, opposition built to be measured, credit at the idea, the swap as the
measurement. The high-five is not flattery, because it comes with the one thing flattery
never brings — **tools that will refuse him too** (`riskcell.sh` refuses *his* ordinal cell
exactly as it refuses mine).

*Falsifier for the verdict:* a clause in the kit that survives only by the architecture's
authority — i.e. a rule with no host value earning it and no tool enforcing it. The
candidate I found is the **wind-down budget (17% / grace 9% / drift ≥9%)**: it is a working
default with a rationale (*"so it does not skew them"*) but no host value named and no
guard — `[MINE, ~55%]` that this is the kit's one self-defending number. If he names the
value it serves, the falsifier dissolves and the verdict firms toward 85%.

## 5. Where the architecture strains (challenges, sourced)

1. **The bearing ratio's denominator is its honest weakness** — the canon says so itself
   (`RISK-CANON.md` §"The honest weakness — and it is in the denominator"): U×F is *"the
   mass weight of total bearing instrumentation, with one end's view simply multiplied."*
   Rung 0 is an ordinal confession; the ratio is only as fine as its coarsest term.
2. **Two signing regimes, both principled** (`.project.yml` notes): the kit claims no
   dependence on cryptographic signing (Part E) and t0 signs anyway. *"Say which one a
   report is quoting."* The same tension as Q435 in c4 — and the same steer applies:
   signing tensions play out in stagnation; build, sign what's worth it.
3. **Seven read-backs still open** (`NOTES-2026-08-28` §QUESTIONS; MEMORY.md §Open): chiefly
   what `[DR]` stands for on his loop page, and the `?` beside TELEMETRY in his own hand.
   The architecture refuses to fill them — a tool never fills its own verdicts — which is
   right, and which means the kit is shipping with a hole its author drew on purpose.
4. **The instances are unreachable from the hub's reader** (not mounted here); the hub's
   claims about t0/t1/t2 are pointers, and a pointer is not a receipt.
5. **A14, three questions from 2026-08-20 never answered**: context isolation, whether
   SAVANNAH v1.0 is the principle source, seam paranoia level (MEMORY.md).
6. **It can be turned off.** Passthrough is a virtue and a vulnerability: a method that
   defers this completely relies on the operator wanting it. There is no "you must call the
   shot" — by design — so its adoption is measured only by whether the deltas keep getting
   written. `[MINE, ~70%]` that DRIFT.log's cadence is the honest adoption metric.
