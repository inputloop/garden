# .arc/2026-09-13-from-fable — what Book 1 was mutated FROM

Fable's persona-kit work as it stood at the moment Opus 5 began Book 1, copied here byte-for-byte before
any mutation, per Corwin's standing instruction to archive before changing. MANIFEST.sha256 pins the
bytes; `sha256sum -c MANIFEST.sha256` re-proves them.

Book 1 (*The Commons at the Table*) takes the d6xd4 entry, the two ladders, the level bands and the
doubles/seven bump discipline from this material, and then departs from it — different dice, different
spine, a different subject and an author of its own. The departure is the point: these files are the
source, not the draft.
