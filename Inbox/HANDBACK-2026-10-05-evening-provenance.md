context-management reference (attention budget, primacy and recency, four buckets, context
poisoning) and your track.
- **The shelf:** four kinds of shelf where C4 has twenty-six drawers: door and ways, words, ideas,
  subjects.
- **Words:** a fixed working set (17), rotated least-recently-used and logged. Each word has four
