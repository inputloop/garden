4. **Personas.** D wants third-person sheets, the "redundancy" renamed and "comrade" retired. A wants personas kept off what users see, and measured. C finds that "reasons, not decisions" reads the literature correctly. They differ in strength, not direction.

## 4. Composite ideation

Each idea pairs a skill of Bryant's with one of Corwin's. None changes Bryant's skill without him: his region, his call.

1. **The prompt stays home.** Bryant's *caterpillar* saves the verbatim prompt into Corwin's *public-stage* local store (`.house/q/`), never into anything that ships. Meanwhile `Assisted-by:` carries the model and version. The house then holds the provenance instead of a vendor link (S01, S12). *Touches keeper-only material:* tonight's saved prompts carry internal GRAIN work, so they stay local unless he clears each one.
2. **Colophon at the push.** Bryant's *colophon* licence-and-makers check runs inside Corwin's *pu1sh* at the `users` level. In return, colophon adopts pu1sh's NOT CHECKED exit for when its second reader cannot run (S07).
3. **Bytes checked where they came in.** Bryant's *ground-truth* (verify against the authoritative source) runs at download time with *pu1sh*'s MISNAMED magic-byte test. A saved block page then never becomes `BE16.png`.
4. **A legend in every bundle.** Bryant's *rosetta* (ship the decoder) is filled by Corwin's *garden-usage* (each coded name in one plain sentence) inside every `make dist` bundle. This serves the program's one graded measure: helping him find things.
5. **The story goes to the log.** Bryant's *parallax* (a cheap log of the agent's point of view each turn) becomes a source for Corwin's *drift* pages. The work then leads with its subject, and the story of its making goes to the journal (his rule of 2026-10-05; S23). *Touches keeper-only material:* a drift page about tonight cites internal GRAIN work, so any page that goes to Bryant needs his clearance item by item.
