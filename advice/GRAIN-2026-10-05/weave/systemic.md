## 1. The weave

### What the four papers agree on

- **Keep the instrument; gate the way out.** All four prefer `pu1sh` to a ban (A §3.4, B §3.3, C §3.2;
  D §3.2 concedes it).
- **Stop the trailer at its source**, the app's attribution setting, before any downstream check (A rec 2,
  B rec 2, C win 1, D win 1).
- **A client hook is a reminder**; only a server-side check binds every pusher (B rec 4, C rec 1).
- **The weak points:** a gate fitted to one vendor and one incident (mostly closed by c4 4af7de1), and the
  "undeclared means advice" default.
- **Self-evaluation.** The instrument wrote most of what is reviewed, these papers too, and leans toward
  its keeper.
- **The tagged history** gets a forward commit, never a rewrite (A rec 7, B rec 3).

### Where they truly disagree, and why

**The one that matters: what the gate keys on.**
- Corwin's rule keys on *audience* (users).
- D keys on *harm*: privacy and provenance findings stop a push at every non-local level, because a public
  history is cloned and scraped whether or not anyone installs it.
- B keys on *visibility* but defers to his rule and offers a census to settle it.
- A keeps audience, wants declaration made an active choice, and warns the other way: findings that fire
  often are soon answered unread. B adds that an over-stopping hook gets `--no-verify`.

Each lens counts harm in a different unit: A, the keeper's attention; B, the stranger who receives the
artifact; C, the mechanism; D, the commons. None of them is wrong. All of them lack one number: how often
each check fires, and how often falsely, on his real histories. That census is cheap and is not in STEPS
(N-SYSTEMIC-1).

**Second: wording now, or measure first.** D would retire "comrade" and rename the redundancy now. A (E5)
and C (personas cite reasons, not decisions) would measure first: they treat wording as a hypothesis about
trust, where D treats it as the cue itself. The restatement is one edit either way.

**Minor: how many gates.** C wants boundary rules in gates; D's census would retire gates that never fire.
The two compose: a gate earns its place at an act that cannot be undone, by firing on real events.

### What each lens sees that the others miss

- **A:** the keeper's end of the supervision loop (S20); "users" as two audiences, installers (MELPA's
  default recipe ships no `.png` or `.log`) and history readers; the load coded names put on a second user.
- **B:** the plumbing (the fail-open range, now fixed); licence lines that disagree in 14 of 26 pack files
  (S07); MELPA's header form (S06); Forgejo ships server hooks off because they run code on the host,
  which is the price of S05.
- **C:** a different nearest control for each fault; rotation needs every use stamped; light-shelf
  experiment B is confounded (S15); heal prompts should carry the failing check (S17); token-optimizer's
  replication is unwitnessed (S16).
- **D:** the vendor as a place data goes (S10, S11); copyright against human contribution (S12); the
  redundancy's common mode (S13); unmeasured energy; ritual.

### What the burn-down changes

- **Facts.** card-games is declared `users`, the repository is `card-game.el`, and the replay flags exactly
  three faults. A's omission and D's third point close for card-games, but not for the default.
- **Method.** Two small readers with different windows caught all eight plants; neither did alone. The
  self-evaluation concern every paper raises now has a cheap, demonstrated answer (S21), paid in
  electricity on Avalon.
- **Its limit.** The local model's false alarms were as confident as its catches, so S21 sits in front of
  the keeper's eye, never in place of a check.

**The floor, applied to S09**. The safety floor says a mechanical check may control an AI and may
never grant it entry. Under fork A, a clean gate run is the last word before an agent's commit reaches
users, which reads as a check granting entry. Fork B, where a human reads the diff and pushes, fits the
floor. *Falsifier:* he rules that "entry" means his own systems, not a public remote.

**S23, priced.**

*Observed:* the 26 pack skill files hold 35,361 words (`wc`), about 1,360 each. The garden has 80 skill
and soft-skill files and about 110 C4 tools (not sized here).

*Judged:* one read of the skill text is about 109,000 words, and the rewrite as many again, the same
order as the burn-down's Haiku rung. Tokens are not the cost. The costs are three. Every file changes at
once, so every reader's model of the garden shifts in a night (A: churn makes the error boundary harder
to learn). Every touched seal is made again. And moving the story out is a judgment per file that must
leave the must-follow forms (licence, provenance) standing.

*The buy:* one machine-readable licence per file, so files can cross into Bryant's garden, whose map
reports a clean `reuse lint`; shippables with no vendor links; work a second reader enters by its subject.
The pass never needs repeating, but only if it ends in a standing gauge (egress 3a, `reuse lint` and LINK
at `make dist`).

*The shape:* the generated parts go garden-wide after S07 and S18; the story comes out of what ships
first, and out of the rest at next touch; S21 reviews.

**The ne'er-do-well's reading**: polishing the sign while the door waits. S23 is the sign, and
card-games and door render are the door. *Falsifier:* 1.0.92 ships before S23's first stage.

## 3. New steps

- **N-SYSTEMIC-1.** Owner: M.
  - *The step:* an advice and false-positive census. Replay the current `pu1sh` read-only over the full
    history of every published and private repository, and count FLAG, ADVICE and false positives per
    check and per level.
  - *Why:* A's E1 and B's experiment 1 are the same run. S08 is a decision about load, and it needs this
    number. It costs hours of compute and none of the keeper's time. It comes before S08.
- **N-SYSTEMIC-2.** Owner: C+M.
  - *The step:* ask once where the signals say users. When a remote missing from `USERS.tsv` carries a
    release tag or a package recipe, `pu1sh` stops and asks for a declaration once instead of grading it
    "published, no known users". D's E2 (14 days of forge clone data) seeds it.
  - *Why:* A's errata keep the second half of A's recommendation 1 standing, and no step from S01 to S27
    carries it. The default is where every lens found the weakest knowledge. His decision comes first,
    because it changes his default.
- **N-SYSTEMIC-3.** Owner: M.
  - *The step:* a firing census (D's E6). For each gate and guard skill, count the real, unplanted firings
    that changed an outcome, from logs already kept (git, the ChangeLog, DRIFT.log, the kid glove's
    ledger), with NOT CHECKED where no log exists.
  - *Why:* it is the only measure of what the apparatus costs the whole against what it buys, and it
    tells S23 what to retire rather than reinvent. It reports only; retiring stays his decision.
