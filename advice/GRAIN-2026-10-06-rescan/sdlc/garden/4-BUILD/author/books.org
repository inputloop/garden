#+title: Books on the author's edition: one row per title and per right

No book text, audio or cover is in this prototype. This is the gate that decides, later, what may
be put on a stick. A publisher holds a print licence for a set number of copies and a set time; it
does not hold the rights. The author holds every other right unless he sold it (audio is often
sold). So the check is made for each title and each right: has the author sold it?

A row may say =yes= under include only when:
- the right is =text=, =audio= or =other=;
- "sold by author" is =no= (not =yes=, not =unknown=);
- someone is named under "checked by" and a date (YYYY-MM-DD) is filled in.

=bin/lr1bk= prints the rows that may go on a stick and refuses the file otherwise. The list of
titles is collected by Jon; whether each right is sold is the author's and his counsel's to say.
Nobody here guesses it. The rows below are placeholders, all =no=.
