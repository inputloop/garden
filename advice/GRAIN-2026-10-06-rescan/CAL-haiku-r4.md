## 2. Problems and opportunities discussed

Governance for collaborative chat systems: both frame this as building space for people to interact safely rather than simply adding features. mplscorwin: solving governance even for chat is a "steep problem" when the goal is a canvas/garden for shared ideas rather than a feature set.

Healthcare structure: vast spending on billing, accounting, and finance rather than care itself. bryant1269 noted a past report recommending funding for staff was rejected. Both agree the problem is legislative, not technical.

Provenance and licensing: how to track authorship and derived works in co-creation, especially if collaborators separate later. mplscorwin emphasizes this is "the cheapest answer from an energy standpoint" and socially responsible. bryant1269 underscores the importance of recording that derived works came from their collaboration.

Co-creation alignment: managing what gets shared, what stays private, and what leaks unintentionally. mplscorwin: the real issue is people share passwords; software can't realistically prevent human behavior.

## 3. Where they agree, and where they diverge

**Agreement:** "A rising tide raises all ships. We move together" (bryant1269). Both endorse pragmatism over idealism, free software as a brake on future greediness, and the principle "no secrets travel." Both value admitting mistakes transparently. Both see safety features as essential to freedom.

**Divergence:** mplscorwin focuses on technical infrastructure and accountability within development. bryant1269emphasizes pragmatism and legislative solutions: "If it's not pragmatic it better damn well be *essential*." bryant1269 also frames healthcare as unsolvable technically; mplscorwin sees potential in communications infrastructure.
