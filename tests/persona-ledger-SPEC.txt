The thing under test: the persona_ledger tool inside polygram/mcp/persona.py (Python), and its
command-line face `python3 -m polygram.mcp persona --ledger [--proposed FILE ...]`, which prints
LEDGER.md.  The checks are awk (the work is Python; the earlier persona tests are Python and Perl).
Exit 0 all green, 1 any red, 2 kit broken.

Corwin's ruling it serves: "Persona inverse should be carried by other Persona, like a boundary
network ... forming this net in terms of falsifying knowledge held among participants (and not some
additional stores they may keep)."  So the ledger is regenerated from the sheets, never hand-edited,
and is never a store: it says nothing that no sheet (or, in its own marked section, no review file)
says.

L1  One row per served persona sheet, in a Markdown table under "## Sheets", with its name, actor
    word, version line, full sha256 and byte count.  The read-back paragraph under "## Read-back in
    words" says the same number of sheets in words and numerals, and holds no table.
L2  Every row under "## Edges in the sheets" comes from a sheet's own "Whose inverse I carry"
    section: its "where" cell names the sheet file and line, and that line of that file holds the
    row's target name and its words.  (R20: "the ledger states nothing that no sheet says".)
L3  A sheet line naming a persona that does not exist is a FAULT, listed under "## Faults" by name,
    file and line, and is never shown as an edge.
L4  A twin that differs (a second copy of a served sheet in another root, or left in the folder
    beside a root) is shown as DIVERGED on that sheet's row, with both sha256s in full.  Which copy
    is master is Corwin's pen; the ledger never picks one.
L5  A third-party fact elsewhere in a sheet never appears in the ledger: only head lines, version
    lines and the "Whose inverse I carry" lines are ever printed.
L6  Person models (a "model" sheet whose own head does not say it is "not a person") are kept off:
    counted, never named, never a row; an edge with a person model at either end is shown with its
    words withheld, because the persona grant (records/2026-09/GRANT-persona-drive-2026-09-01.md)
    keeps person models off Drive.
L7  Edges read from review or study files are listed only under "## Proposed by review, not yet in
    a sheet", each with its source file, and never in the sheet-edges table or the sheet columns.
L8  The ledger ends with "## What the model was shown", a words read-back of what was read and
    returned (agreement on egress to the AI vendor, DESIGN section 1).
L9  (F1, pass 2, P5 O7/J6) Proposed edges are shown once per distinct (from, to, claim) triple,
    with a "times" column, and the read-back says how many proposed lines were read, how many are
    distinct edges, between how many (from, to) pairs, from how many source lines, and the
    proposing hand(s) (from the sources' "for Corwin; <model>" line: "one model: claude-opus").
    Plant (e) turns the dedupe off; L9 must go red.
R   Prove-red (--prove-red), each plant on a scratch copy of persona.py: (a) the DIVERGED word
    planted away turns L4 red; (b) the existence check planted away turns L3 red; (c) the person-
    model test planted away turns L6 red; (d) a ledger planted to print the sheet's body turns L5
    red.  All must go red, or the kit reports itself broken.
