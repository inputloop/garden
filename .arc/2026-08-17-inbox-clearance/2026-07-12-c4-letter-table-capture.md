## Instrument's reading (observation, not part of the capture)

17 letters × 4 slots. The columns look roughly like: instance · aggregate/
structure · operation · context/class — four representations of one concern
per row. Read against SEAMS 0.2: every pair of columns in a row is a
candidate seam (two representations of one truth that can silently split).
The table doesn't state doctrine; it maps where the doctrine applies.
S13 (signed directive ↔ binding authority) is the C4-transit row already in
the catalog. Related: book/C4.md ("the seam is the leverage"), models/c4/,
memory/c4-ambitions.md.
