# c4-consent-quartet — 4 of the 17 to scope consent into behavior templates

**C4 = `ghostwheel.fn.sh`**, your hub-and-spoke mesh with the 17-letter
filesystem-as-DB taxonomy: asset · command · document · environment · feature ·
graph · heap · identity · job · link · mapping · object · package · register ·
subscription · taxonomy · workspace.

You said only **four** should be required to scope consent meaningfully into a
behavior template (a shared persona/skill artifact over ccspt/ghostwheel). I will
**not** guess your framework into existence — here is a *proposal* to confirm or
swap, not a decision:

| dim | letter | role in consent-scoping |
|-----|--------|--------------------------|
| WHO | **identity** | the principal consenting (whose model/behavior this is) |
| WHAT | **package** | the behavior-template artifact being shared (the `.skill` bundle) |
| GRANT | **subscription** | the consent relationship itself: scope, terms, revocation |
| PROOF | **register** | signed/SHA-256 provenance that the consent happened |

Reads as: *WHO consents to share WHAT, under WHICH GRANT, with WHAT PROOF.*
Plausible swaps you might prefer: **link** (the spoke seam) for transport, or
**mapping** (scope/projection) in place of subscription.

**Action when confirmed:** fold the chosen four into `model.md` as the consent
dimension-set, and reference from `Bryant-MTB-Stub.md` and the dist/ccspt seam.
