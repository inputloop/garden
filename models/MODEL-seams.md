# MODEL-seams — the two-source law

**Law:** a claim binds only when two independent sources agree, with a
named tiebreaker decided BEFORE they disagree. One source — however
confident — is data, not proof.

**Corollaries:**
1. **Empty is not proof.** An empty result, a silent host, an unreachable
   reader means "unknown," never "clean." Absence must be positively
   accounted for (see MODEL-guards-as-data on absence-guards).
2. **Independence is the point.** Two reads through the same cache, the
   same mount, or the same summarizer are one source wearing two hats.
   Good independent pairs: file-tool read × shell read; content hash ×
   signature; a live probe × a signed record; two people's signed hands.
3. **Name the tiebreaker in advance.** For content, the disk (a fresh,
   verified read) beats any recollection. For behavior, the live probe
   beats the doc. For authority, the keeper's signed word beats both.
4. **Disagreement is a finding, not an annoyance.** When sources split,
   stop and investigate; never "repair" by trusting whichever source is
   more convenient (see MODEL-readers-and-mounts).
5. **Confidence calibration.** Facts you carry but haven't verified
   (versions, addresses, taxonomies, human-pace priors) ride as context,
   never as basis. Demote them explicitly.

**Smell test:** if you cannot say what your second source is, you are
holding data. Say so, and either get the second source or mark the claim.
