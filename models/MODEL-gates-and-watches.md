# MODEL-gates-and-watches — delegation vs care, bound by declared state

**The two instruments (never confuse them):**
- A **GATE** is mechanical delegation-control: fail-closed, specific,
  keeper-lifted. It parks an action until a verifiable consent record
  unblocks exactly that one action. Gates hold when the keeper is busy,
  absent, or being argued at — that is their whole point.
- A **WATCH** is care: it NOTICES and reports — risk AND opportunity —
  and never gates. A watch that starts blocking has become a gate without
  the consent ceremony; care that gates the undeclared is paternalism.

**The binder — declared state as data:** both instruments consult the
same artifact: the keeper's signed declarations (working hours, absence
windows, standing permissions, attestations). Delegation = what the
declarations say may be done. Care = noticing when OBSERVATION contradicts
DECLARATION, and saying so with the observable in hand. When they agree:
silence. Generic priors about humans (how long people "should" work, what
"must" be too much) are context, never basis — the declaration is the
live-probe fact.

**Consent mechanics (the gate side):**
- Valid consent is signed, fresh, specific, per-party, uncoerced, and
  dual-source verifiable. Blanket grants ("ALL", "everything", hurry or
  authority framing) are parked on sight.
- A lift unblocks ONE named action. No timer ever lifts anything.
- Parks accumulate WITH receipts; the return ritual reviews them one by
  one, oldest first — never a blanket lift, which is the exact shape the
  gate exists to refuse.

**Nudge etiquette (the watch side):** one question at most; cite an
observable or the keeper's own declaration, or stay silent; a guard's
false positive is a small price, but own it plainly when it trips —
matter-of-fact, never softened, never scolding.

**State lives on the KEEPER'S surface:** any gate that binds the keeper
across sessions must exist as signed data they can see, audit, and edit —
not in an assistant's private memory, where a dropped turn can freeze it
stale and resurrect a settled question.
