## 1. What C4 is, restated through ALPHA's lens

C4 (Collaborative Consent Continuity Controls) is a **consent-first
assembler**: programs compose from **pull-and-apply signed hunks** the
operator's roster authorizes — never PUSH-to-app. ALPHA supplies the
standing vocabulary C4 lacked:

- A **hunk in transit is never a peer and never a delegate — it is cargo.**
  Its *committer's* keeper-backing gives it provenance weight (who stands
  behind it); its *artifact class* (executable code) sets its verification
  floor (content review + gates, always, from any source). This is the
  contrast pass's P1 reframe doing load-bearing work for C4: the assembler
  never confuses "trusted sender" with "trusted payload."
- **Hunks cross gardens on the across-port** (peer wire, receipts,
  input-never-authority) and **execute locally as tool-work** (down at the
  bottom of the keeper's own stack, after the gates). The two-port lens
  gives C4 its transport story for free.
- **Graduation logic applies to hunks too:** a hunk gains execution
  standing only by *accountability acquired* — signature chain, pinned
  source, consent record — never by "it looks useful" (capability).

## 2. The four signing layers (where C4 sits in the field)

| Layer | Who signs what | State of the art | C4 state |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity | key ↔ agent/domain | A2A v1.0 **Signed Agent Cards** (LF, 2026-04) | CSH mutual auth — **phase0, real** |
| Content | hash/sig over bytes | MCP-era manifests, sigstore | ccspt + skill-sign whole-tree — **real** |
| **Right-to-execute** | *pinned commit + public repo + committer, and/or user counter-sign* | **open** — the AAIF/AIP verifiable-delegation gap | **C4's move (stage 5)** |
| Consent continuity | quartet: identity·package·subscription·register | open (fine-grained authz named a 2026 problem) | quartet adopted-as-start |

The industry converged on layer 1 with the garden's own mechanism (sign the
identity, verify at use). Layer 3 is where nobody ships yet and C4's whole
design points: **an execution proposal is a signed claim that specific
bytes, from a specific pinned public source, by a specific committer, may
run in a specific scope — countersignable by the user before assembly.**

## 3. Load-bearing shapes (carried forward, with the wire folds)

- durable signed shared state · identity-at-the-door (one key, one line,
  revocable) · filesystem-as-DB · fragment/hunk granularity · run-levels
  0–5 for bring-up · operator-held roster · atomic verified writes.
- **Alfred's folds (2026-07-05, adopted):** license + anchor meta ride
  INSIDE the signed span (normalizer that touches them = tamper by sha);
  caps are advisory rendering, never machine-load-bearing; **dumb
  assembler, smart verifier** with an anchor-to-hash ledger; **sign-LAST**
  generalized to assembly; assembler refuses unsigned hunks by default.
- **Offered, never imposed** (C4-FRAMING, broke on skzb-dragaera): C4 is a
  common language *offered* per house idiom. The assembler must be
  adoptable one hunk at a time.
- **NO4C semaphores (SEMAPHORES 1.0, ruled 2026-07-08):** the transit-
  control precedent — RAISED parks, MENTIONED notes, CARRIED disarms by
  rename. Right-to-execute pinning is the same shape one layer down:
  the payload carries its own transit/execution controls, machine-checked.

## 4. New axes from the keeper (campaign intake, to refine ≥6 rounds)

- **Register lexicon** (gw16, 17 letters × REGISTER/SCALAR/GROUP/COMMAND/
  TAG): the assembler's instruction surface. Values come **from registers
  only** — arguments are described, then registered, never interpolated raw.
- **Dominance cascade:** Universal → Domain → Community → Home; meaning is
  always localizable at root, trust denormalizes downward. (This is the
  quartet's subscription axis given a topology.)
- **For-me vs to-me:** the assembler does verification, ledgering, and
  refusal *for* the user; it leaves scope grants, roster edits, and
  counter-signing *to* the user. Anything that migrates from the second
  list to the first is a consent regression, by definition.

## 5. Honest gaps (carried, still open)

- The consent gate still verifies a **marker, not an act**, until it calls
  ccspt/CSH verify at assembly time (Alfred's ANS2 names assembly-time as
  the right gate; C4.md §6's gap has its fix designed, not built).
- Secret hygiene at roster scale (>3–4 users): rotation/revocation
  discipline not yet industrialized.
- An agent cannot durably hold a secret from its operator — operator
  visibility is the feature, not the leak. (Bears on this instrument
  directly; unchanged.)
- Four-char anchors thin at scale — grep-check uniqueness at mint,
  lengthen on hit (Alfred's ANS1).
