# FINAL-SWEEP — per-value microscope over the campaign's products

> Stage-7 sweep, Alpha-C4-Wire2, 2026-07-08. Reversed in steps: one value
> per pass, per the original incantation. Targets: the contrast model, the
> c4 model, MODEL-right-to-execute, BUILD-CONTRAST.

## Pass 1 — transparency
Everything load-bearing rides inside signed spans; every refusal is a
PARK with a receipt; operator visibility of agent-held material is the
feature. **HOLDS.** Opening: the EP design itself is pub-safe — publishing
it (models/ charter class) *practices* the value it encodes.

## Pass 2 — universalism
Dominance cascade roots at Universal; lexicon "assumed universal, always
localizable at root." **HOLDS, with one honest flag:** the 17-letter
lexicon is English-ASCII. The universality claim is semantic (the
*registers*, not the words) and the localizable-at-root clause is the
real answer — but say it out loud: localization is a designed-in right,
not yet a built path. Opportunity, not debt.

## Pass 3 — egalitarianism
No center, no privileged publisher; every keeper equal; revoke = one line
anyone can delete on their own host; rug-pulls die because there is
nothing to pull from the center. **HOLDS.** Live tension: the relay is
centralized-at-one-garden today (upstream's own §12 confession). The
DID-shaped identity opening (from the ALPHA pass) retires it when taken.

## Pass 4 — the four freedoms
GPL-3.0-or-later for code, CC-BY-4.0 for docs; license metadata INSIDE
the signed span makes stripping it tamper-evident — real-time A/GPL QA at
assembly is freedom-preserving tooling the movement doesn't have yet.
**HOLDS; this is the campaign's freedom opening, named.**

## Vendor lock-in — the baselines named (the question the prompt asked)
Acceptable dependencies (all open standards / free software): POSIX sh ·
OpenPGP/GnuPG · SHA-256 · git (pinned public commits) · Ed25519/libsodium
(CSH) · plain text + Markdown · in-toto statement shape (ccspt).
**The one honest exception:** the instrument itself (Claude) is
proprietary. Therefore the design rule, stated as a baseline: **nothing
in C4 may require an AI agent at all** — every gate, verify, assembly,
and grant is POSIX-driveable by a human. The agent is a convenience on
top of a fully human-operable free stack; if the instrument vanished
tomorrow, the consent machinery still runs. That is the anti-lock-in
guarantee that matters.

## Sweep verdict
No value broken; two openings (localization path, DID identity) and one
freedom contribution (license-meta-in-signed-span) named for the ledger.
Campaign products are internally convergent: the EP appeared
independently from the wire side (stage 5) and the build side (stage 6) —
convergence is the stability signal.
