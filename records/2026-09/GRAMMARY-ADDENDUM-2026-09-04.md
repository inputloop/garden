## The trait (new, and it is the load-bearing one)

- **own-error-is-the-relaxable-layer** — make the layer most likely to be *your* error the layer
  that relaxes, gets marked, or gets made explicit; never the layer you defend hardest. Short
  form: *ink the world's facts, pencil your own reasoning.* **Found, not designed** — it converged
  independently in five rooms on the 2026-09-03 crawl. It is the floor
  (`governance-lineage-walk`) read right-side-up: ground objections in a traceable lineage, *and
  the rung you relax first is your own*.
  *Owner:* the `pencil-your-own` mint. *Consumer:* `scripts/pencil.sh`.
- **the three refusals** — the fence that stops the trait becoming an excuse: (1) only what is
  **yours**, never another party's ink; (2) never the **floor**; (3) **marked, not deleted**.
  *Consumer:* `pencil.sh` rules 1–2 and the record-diff half of its XX pair.

## Disposition verdicts (labels — consumer: `pencil.sh`)

A concern's disposition, first-match-wins, and the order is the design:

`FLOOR` (never relaxes, checked first so a floor item cannot be dismissed for want of a falsifier) ·
`NOT-YOURS` (another party holds the ink — route it) · `HOLD` (your record, not your claim) ·
`FEELING` (no falsifier; label it and move) · `STANDS` (two independent witnesses; nothing of yours
left to relax) · `PENCIL` (yours, single-sourced — proceed and **name** it).

## Measurement (labels — consumer: `gauge.sh`)

- **countable-gauge** — a cheap standing ratio that catches *analysis wearing a write-up jacket*.
  Before asserting a document is useful/accurate/careful/improved: name the ratio that would show
  it, then print the ratio. Measured case: 58 framework-mentions per reader-affordance; the rebuild
  scored 3.0.
- **two-source dagger (`†`)** — mark a claim with no independent second witness rather than imply
  earned confidence. A wrong correction is worse than the original error, so a correction must
  itself clear the two-source bar. *Consumer:* the `unwitnessed-claim` gauge.
- **`INF` as signal** — a zero denominator is not an error, it is the loudest result available:
  the document affords the reader nothing at all on that axis.
- **no threshold, by design** — the gauge prints counts and ratios and never a verdict. A threshold
  picked-not-validated is the same fault the gauge exists to catch.

## The planning method (labels — consumer: `buckets.sh`)

- **Fortress Planning ⇄ the Planning Palace** (also Sea/See Fortress, C4ortress, C4ort) — the wide
  ideation walk. Palace = a mind-palace in gravityless n-D space; each GIVENS group a disjoint
  level, connection visible only when you pick the dimensions that matter.
- **GIVENS buckets (a)–(i)** — nine *shapes of problem*, walked hardest-first (i → a).
- **the fill invariant** — no second entry in any group until every group has one. Checked against
  **arrival order**, because a walk can end well-shaped and still have been filed wrong; final
  counts alone cannot see it.
- **the stop rule** — all-but-two groups filled twice, **or** no unique ideation left locally. Two
  groups may honestly stay at one; padding the floor with a weak second entry fakes it.
- **stop-on-this-corpus** — a stop is met on what you walked, never absolutely. Record the corpus
  beside the verdict or it is a stop nobody can check. *(Prose, not a label — no consumer yet.)*

## Portable notes (labels — consumer: `pmnotes.sh`)

`OK` · `GUESS` · `NO-TRIGGERS` · `UNNAMED-SOURCE` · `UNSOURCED` · `UNFALSIFIABLE`. The last two fail
a notes file; the rest are hygiene. **From:** (where it came from) and **Would-change:** (what would
prove it wrong) are the two habits that make a handoff export memory rather than confident-sounding
text.

## Testing idiom (labels — consumer: every `tests.sh`)

- **prove-red** — `sh tests.sh --prove-red` plants a deliberately wrong expectation and asserts the
  suite reports it. *If you cannot demonstrate the check going red, it is not a check* — a green
  suite that cannot fail is indistinguishable from one that is not running.
- **cold-unpack** — test a bundle extracted into an empty directory with nothing from the source
  tree in scope, so what passed is the bundle and not the working copy. The operational form of
  **self-contained universe**.
- **oracle-transfers** *(already canon; now has consumers)* — the reusable asset is the
  fixtures + expected verdicts, never the code. Every mint's `fixtures/expected.tsv` is the oracle;
  the `.sh` is disposable.
