## Loop 2 · the two cold readers, and the one redesign

Two readers, neither shown the design notes. Both are quoted in full in this session's transcript.

**The game master's finding, and it is the important one.** All four books' back matter ran *the same
rhetorical move in the same order* — the dice handed the author a bad trait, the author refuses to
disown it, the refusal proves the book's thesis. Their words: *"that's not two authors converging
independently; that's one hand"*, and worse, *"the persona absorbs the criticism the rules deserved"* —
Book 3 shipped with four dead ends and spent its author's-note budget on being competitive.

**REDESIGN TRIGGERED (the one allowed; loop count reset).** The back-matter architecture. Each book now
declares a `kind` and the four kinds must differ: **disclosure** (Vanth states a conflict of interest and
explicitly declines to make anything of the dice), **refusal** (Pell declines the assignment and talks
about the man who could not see the red pips), **dismissal** (Roux-Abara gives it three sentences and
returns to her errata), **reflection** (Ilunga keeps it, because Hope *is* his mechanic, and turns it
into the independence check rather than a virtue).

That gate is now mechanical: `gb1md --voices` FAULTs a set whose back-matter kinds repeat, with the
message *"a set whose authors all make the same move is one author in masks."* A cold reader had to be
hired to apply it once; now it runs.
